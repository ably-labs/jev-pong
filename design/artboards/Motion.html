Jev Pong motion notes

Durations in ms unless stated. Linear means linear: the speed of the ball is the data.

01Ball, in the clip

At t = 0 the scripted serve tweens the ball one step out of centre over 300 ease-out, in all four lanes at once. Every later step is a linear tween whose duration is exactly that decision's recorded latency, timed to arrive as the decision lands. No easing inside a step. GPT's ball sits still on its serve step until 3776.

02Ball, on the site

The home replay is driven by the recording and behaves exactly as 01. Live play and watch: on each snapshot, tween the ball to its new position over min(latency, 1200), linear. Paddles ease-out over 120 to the decided y. Score changes never interpolate.

03Trail

Length follows speed. On the clip court trail_px = clamp(260 × 430 / latency, 24, 260); scale with court width elsewhere. Seven discs behind the ball: radius r → 0.35r, opacity 0.5 → 0.07, spacing growing 12% per disc, all in the ball's colour. No blur, no glow. Trail samples the actual path, so it bends at a bounce.

04Latency number

Shows max(lastLatency, elapsedInFlight). A steady lane reads steady; a slower-than-last decision climbs; the first decision has no last, so it counts up from 0 in the in-flight style (weight 400, --fg-inflight, unit too). On landing: snap to the measured value, back to weight 500 and the lane colour, and pulse: scale 1 → 1.06 → 1 over 180 (60 out, 120 in), colour flashes to pure white or black for 120. The unit does not pulse. Update per frame, not per ms.

05Counters and badges

The decisions · returns line increments with a 120 opacity dip, no bounce. Status badges (serving, point, game over, out of credits) fade in 150, hold, fade out 150; they never slide and never push the layout.

06Presence (the Ably moment)

The live dot breathes: opacity 1 → 0.4 → 1 over 2000 ease-in-out. A new feed line ("Jev joined") fades in over 200, is bold for 3000, then settles to muted. Watcher count and dots cross-fade in 150; a dot is added or removed per member, capped at six plus a count.

07End card timing

At 12.0 s freeze all four courts for 400, cross-fade 300 to the end card, bars grow 0 → length over 600 ease-out staggered 80 top to bottom, numbers count up in sync. Hold 2500. Clip length 15.2 s. Loop is a hard cut to the opening frame, no fade. The static image and OG image are the held frame.

08Opening and reduced motion

No intro animation: frame 1 is the finished layout with four balls at centre and four numbers at 0. With prefers-reduced-motion: no trail, no pulse, no breathing dot, ball jumps per snapshot; the numbers still count up, since that is information, not decoration.

Must not get wrong
aBall speed is data. Ease nothing inside a step; only the serve and the paddles ease.
bLanes are ordered by measured latency once, at record time, and never re-sort.
cOrange is Jev's ball, trail, number and bar. Not paddles, not buttons, not the live dot, not the name.
dThe number is the largest thing in the row at every width. If the phone row cannot fit 34 px, drop the name to a second line, not the number.
eTabular figures on every mono number so right-aligned columns do not jitter while counting.
fPowered by Ably on every surface with clearspace; the real SVG replaces the dashed box at the same height, never scaled to fit text.
gNo horizontal scroll at 390. Live games are a list on phone, cards on desktop.
hCopy stays anti-hype: "typed-decision model", never "can't hallucinate", never vendor latency claims. The measurement line is one string in one place.
